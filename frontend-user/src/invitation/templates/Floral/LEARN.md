## Style summary

- Pink palette: `bg-pink-50`, `text-pink-800`, `border-pink-200`.
- Big floral emoji at the top: `<div className="text-6xl">🌸</div>`.
- White cards with thick pink border: `border-4 border-pink-200`.

---

## Same contract

Like all templates, it accepts `data` with the standard shape (`groomName`, `brideName`, `date`, `location`, `story`, `schedule`).

The only thing that changes between templates is the **visual treatment**. Same data, different feeling.

---

## Tailwind colors

A quick guide to Tailwind color classes:
- `bg-pink-50` — very light pink background.
- `text-pink-800` — dark pink text.
- `border-pink-200` — light pink border.

The number `(50, 100, 200, ..., 900)` is the shade. Lower = lighter, higher = darker.
